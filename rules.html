Objective:

The goal of Blackjack is to have a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer without exceeding 21.

Card Values:

  • Number cards (2-10) are worth their face value.
  • Face cards (King, Queen, Jack) are worth 10 points.
  • Aces can be worth 1 or 11 points, depending on which benefits the player most.

Game Setup:

  1. The game is typically played with one or more standard 52-card decks.
  2. Each player, including the dealer, is dealt two cards.
  3. The players’ cards are usually face-up, while the dealer has one card face-up and one face-down (the "hole" card).

Winning and Losing:

  • If a player’s hand exceeds 21, they "bust" and automatically lose.
  • If the dealer busts, all remaining players win.
  • If the player's total is higher than the dealer’s without busting, they win.
  • If the dealer's total is higher, the player loses.
  • If both have the same total, it’s a push (tie), and the player's bet is returned.
  • A Blackjack (Ace + 10-point card) wins automatically, unless the dealer also has one (resulting in a push).

Gameplay:

  1. Player's Turn:
    • Players can choose to:
      • Hit – Take another card.
      • Stand – Keep their current hand.
      • Double Down – Double their bet, take one more card, and then stand.
      • Split – If they have two identical cards, they can split them into two separate hands (this requires an additional bet).
  2. Dealer's Turn:
    • The dealer reveals their face-down card
    • If the dealer’s hand is 16 or lower, they must hit.
    • If the dealer’s hand is 17 or higher, they must stand (some casinos require the dealer to hit on a "soft 17" – a hand including an Ace valued as 11).
