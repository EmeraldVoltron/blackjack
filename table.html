Player's Actions

Player's Options

  • Hit - request another card from the dealer in an attempt to improve your hand. You can continue hitting until you decide to stand or your hand exceeds 21, which results in a bust.

  • Stand - you choose not to take any additional cards. When you stand, you are satisifed with the value of your hand and signal that you want to hold your current total, allowing the dealer to play their hand next.

  • Split - seperate a pair of identical cards into two seperate hands, placing an additional bet equal to your original wager. Each new hand is then played individually, following the standard rules of the game.
